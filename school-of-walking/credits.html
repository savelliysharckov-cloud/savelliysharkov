Credits

Who made the school, which films it watched, and what it used to watch them.

School of Walking
Saveliy Sharkov: concept, research, direction. Made during the Master in Applied AI for Arts and Design (MAIAD), Elisava, Barcelona, 2026.
Restaurant, USA (1946)
Archive film, 1946. Faces pixelated.
Germany (1944)
Newsreel footage, Germany, 1944. Rights in German newsreels of this period may be held by Transit Film GmbH. Shown for research and teaching, without commercial use.
Ice-cream shop and Restaurant, Japan
Stock footage from Pexels, used under the Pexels licence. Faces pixelated.
Toronto (1928)
Archive street film, 1928. Shown only as skeleton data: the home page replay, the gait atlases, Plate 6.
Munich (2020s)
Street recording of a pedestrian zone. Shown only as skeleton data: a gait atlas and Plate 6.
Tyumen, pedestrian crossing, winter
Video published under a Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike licence. Shown as Plate 3, The Rhythm of Walking.
Rennes, INRIA, May 2009
Crowd experiment recorded at INRIA, Rennes. Shown as Plate 5, Counterflow, and as the machine's view in the Lab.
Ljubljana, Stari trg, 18 July 2019
Street recording. Shown only as the machine's view, in the Lab.
Room photographs
The interiors in the wallpaper mock-ups are stock photographs, the venue rooms from Pexels.
Machine
Ultralytics YOLO26 pose and segmentation models, ByteTrack, MTCNN (facenet-pytorch), OpenCV, FFmpeg.
Type
Overpass and Overpass Mono (SIL Open Font License), on the site; Lora, on the plates.
