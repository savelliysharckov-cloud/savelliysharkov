Entrance exam

Before the first lesson the school needs your tempo. Walk on the spot at the pace you walk to the shop. Press the space bar, or click the circle, each time a foot lands. Twenty steps are enough.

The exam measures only the time between your taps. Nothing leaves your browser.

Your tempo is compared with 23 passers-by from the archive: fourteen in Toronto in 1928 and nine in Munich today. A version that reads your rhythm from your body through the camera is in preparation.

School of Walking is a project by Saveliy Sharkov, made during the Master in Applied AI for Arts and Design at Elisava, Barcelona, 2026.
