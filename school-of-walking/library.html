Library

The school's ancestors, from a dictionary of gestures in 1832 to stopwatches on the pavements of 31 cities. And two neighbours in Barcelona who work close by.

Ancestors

  • 1832Andrea de JorioLa mimica degli antichi investigata nel gestire napoletano

    A dictionary of the gestures of Naples. A model for a dictionary of walks.

  • 1887Eadweard MuybridgeAnimal Locomotion

    Hundreds of plates of people walking, photographed in sequence. Walking cut into frames for the first time.

  • 1894Étienne-Jules MareyLe Mouvement

    Chronophotography: a whole walk on one plate. The Traces wallpaper descends from it.

  • 1895Wilhelm Braune and Otto FischerDer Gang des Menschen

    The first three-dimensional measurement of a human walk, joint by joint.

  • 1911Frank B. GilbrethMotion Study

    Light bulbs on workers' hands, long exposures, the efficient movement. Measurement as management.

  • 1934Marcel MaussTechniques of the Body

    Walking is learned: from mothers, from regiments, from films. The theory behind the school.

  • 1947Rudolf Laban and F. C. LawrenceEffort

    A notation for the quality of movement, not only its path.

  • 1963Erving GoffmanBehavior in Public Places

    Civil inattention: the short look between strangers that the machine's rule erases.

  • 1970Monty PythonThe Ministry of Silly Walks

    A government department that funds new ways of walking. The school's comic ancestor.

  • 1971Jan GehlLife Between Buildings

    Cities planned around people walking and staying, observed by counting them.

  • 1974Marcel JousseL'Anthropologie du geste

    Memory and thinking carried by gesture and rhythm.

  • 1980William H. WhyteThe Social Life of Small Urban Spaces

    Time-lapse films of New York plazas. Observation of walkers as a method.

  • 1999Robert Levine and Ara NorenzayanThe Pace of Life in 31 Countries

    Pedestrians timed over 60 feet in 31 cities. Walking speed as a measure of a culture.

  • 1999Maxine Sheets-JohnstoneThe Primacy of Movement

    Movement comes before language and thought.

Neighbours

Two Barcelona studios use machine vision on bodies and archives. The school owes them a look, and states plainly where it differs.

Estampa

A Barcelona collective that turns machine vision on archives and on itself: object detection run over early film, skeletons drawn over archive photographs in Paisanajes, machine descriptions of images in Ekphrasis.

Shared
Archive film read by a detector; skeletons instead of people.
Different
Estampa labels and describes what is in a frame. The school only measures walking over time, and turns it into lessons, rules and wallpaper.

Domestic Data Streamers

Uncoded Gestures (2025), at the Escoleta in Poblenou: a camera reads a visitor's pose and finds the closest gesture in thousands of archive photographs from Hospitalet and Poblenou, shown side by side with the visitor.

Shared
Live people set next to archive bodies; a caption of place and year; Mauss among the references.
Different
They match a pose in one frame. The school's entrance exam matches a rhythm over twenty steps.

School of Walking is a project by Saveliy Sharkov, made during the Master in Applied AI for Arts and Design at Elisava, Barcelona, 2026.
