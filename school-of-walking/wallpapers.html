Wallpapers

The machine's drawings repeat well. Each wallpaper is a 53 × 53 cm repeat built from one film: who walked, where they went, where they looked, how often they drank.

1 m. The figure is 1.7 m tall, drawn from a passer-by in Toronto, 1928.
Motif

Colourway

In rooms

Walk of your venue

A wallpaper made from your own room. With your permission the school films one ordinary hour in your café, bar, shop or museum. The machine hides every face and draws the walking, the glances and the sips of that hour into a repeat for your walls.

To commission one, write to savelsharkov@gmail.com.
