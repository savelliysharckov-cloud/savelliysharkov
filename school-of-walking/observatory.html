Observatory

Four films, each seen twice. On the left is the footage. On the right is everything the machine kept of it. Choose Wipe and drag across a film to move between the two, or Overlay to lay the machine's drawing over the footage.

Restaurant, USA (1946)

A kitchen and a dining room. The machine follows heads, hands and feet.

Restaurant, USA (1946)Machine reading, Barcelona (2026)
0:00

Archive film. Faces are pixelated in the footage. The machine sees seventeen points per body and nothing else: the head ray runs from the middle of the ears through the nose, and the routes are drawn on the floor under each body.

  • a bone between two of the 17 points
  • the route under a body, a dot every 22 cm
  • a look that lands on another head
  • a sip: a hand at the mouth

Germany (1944)

A Nazi parade, drawn white on black.

Germany (1944)Machine reading, Barcelona (2026)
0:00

Newsreel footage. Here the machine draws bones, head rays and a ring each time a foot lands. The tags above the heads are track numbers.

The film shows a parade of the Nazi party. It is here as evidence of how walking can be drilled, next to the lesson on Formation. Its sound is off until you turn it on.

  • a bone between two of the 17 points
  • a head ray, from the ears through the nose
  • a foot landing
