Artist, filmmaker and curator working with moving and still image, installation, performance, sound and food — increasingly through generative AI. Interested in what reverse-engineering the human reveals: that we, too, run on input we did not fully choose.

Exhibited at CCA Tbilisi; winner in video dance & film at the Contemporary Dance & Experimental Art Festival. Former art director at Schrocher Gallery and programme director at Dust.tbilisi. Founder of Capsule, a time-locked digital storage platform.

From September 2026 — MA in Applied AI for Arts and Design, Elisava, Barcelona.
